Motion

1.2

Definitions

Speed distance travelled per unit time.
Velocity speed in a given direction.
Acceleration change in velocity per unit time.

Formulas

v = s ÷ t speed = distance ÷ time · average speed = total distance ÷ total time
a = Δv ÷ Δt acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time taken
v = u + at  ·  s = ut + ½at²  ·  v² = u² + 2as equations of uniform acceleration
g ≈ 9.8 m/s² acceleration of free fall near the Earth's surface (≈ constant)

Graphs

Distance–time — gradient gives speed; a flat line means at rest.
Speed–time — gradient gives acceleration; area under the line gives distance.

Mass & Weight

1.3

Definitions

Mass a measure of the quantity of matter in an object.
Weight a gravitational force on an object that has mass.
Gravitational field strength (g) force per unit mass; equivalent to the acceleration of free fall.

Formula

W = mg  ·  g = W ÷ m weight = mass × gravitational field strength (unit: N)

Density

1.4

Definition

Density (ρ) mass per unit volume.

Formula

ρ = m ÷ V density = mass ÷ volume (unit: kg/m³ or g/cm³)

Forces

1.5

Definitions

Spring constant (k) force per unit extension.
Limit of proportionality the point on a load–extension graph beyond which the graph is no longer a straight line (Hooke's law no longer applies).
Moment force × perpendicular distance from the pivot — a measure of the turning effect of a force.
Centre of gravity the point where the weight of an object appears to act.
Equilibrium no resultant force and no resultant moment acting on an object.

Formulas

F = ma resultant force = mass × acceleration (same direction)
k = F ÷ x spring constant = force ÷ extension (Hooke's law)
moment = F × d force × perpendicular distance from the pivot (principle of moments: clockwise = anticlockwise)

Graph

Load–extension — straight line obeys Hooke's law; L marks the limit of proportionality.

Momentum

1.6

Definitions

Momentum (p) mass × velocity.
Impulse force × time for which the force acts (= change in momentum).

Formulas

p = mv momentum = mass × velocity
impulse = FΔt = Δ(mv) impulse = change in momentum
F = Δp ÷ Δt resultant force = change in momentum per unit time

Energy, Work & Power

1.7

Definitions

Work done energy transferred when a force moves an object (= F × d).
Power work done per unit time (energy transferred per unit time).
Efficiency useful energy output ÷ total energy input (× 100%).

Formulas

Ek = ½mv² kinetic energy
ΔEp = mgΔh change in gravitational potential energy
W = Fd = ΔE work done = force × distance moved (in the direction of the force)
P = W ÷ t = ΔE ÷ t power = work done ÷ time
efficiency = useful output ÷ total input (× 100% for a percentage)

Pressure

1.8

Definition

Pressure force per unit area.

Formulas

p = F ÷ A pressure = force ÷ area (unit: Pa = N/m²)
Δp = ρgΔh change in pressure beneath a liquid surface = density × g × depth
