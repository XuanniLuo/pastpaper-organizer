Kinetic Particle Model of Matter

2.1

States of matter

Solid — particles tightly packed in a regular lattice, vibrate about fixed positions.
Liquid — particles close but disordered, slide past one another.
Gas — particles far apart, moving fast and randomly.

Definitions

Internal energy the kinetic energy and potential energy of the molecules (particles) of the object📌 考过 · 42/F/M/26 Q5 温度降低时内能如何变化 · 43/M/J/22 Q4 烟粒——内能与压强. A rise in temperature increases the average kinetic energy of the particles, so internal energy rises.
Temperature a measure of the average kinetic energy of the particles in a substance📌 考过 · 42/F/M/26 Q5 粒子平均动能与温度的关系.
Absolute zero the lowest possible temperature (−273 °C = 0 K), at which the particles have the least kinetic energy📌 考过 · 43/O/N/23 Q4 定义绝对零度 + 开尔文↔摄氏度换算.
Gas pressure caused by particles colliding with the walls of the container, exerting a force per unit area. Faster particles (higher temperature) or a smaller volume → more frequent, harder collisions → higher pressure.📌 考过 · 42/F/M/26 Q5 用粒子解释升温时气压变化 · 41/O/N/22 Q4 被困气体——压强与功 · 42/M/J/24 Q4 气压与温度
Brownian motion the random motion of (smoke) particles caused by collisions with the (much smaller, fast-moving) molecules of the air or liquid — evidence for the kinetic particle model📌 考过 · 43/M/J/22 Q4 烟粒随机运动 · 41/M/J/26 Q5 灰尘粒子随机运动.

Formulas

T (K) = θ (°C) + 273 Convert between kelvin and degrees Celsius.
pV = constant Boyle's law — fixed mass of gas at constant temperature.📌 考过 · 43/O/N/23 Q4 体积翻倍求新压强 · 42/O/N/25 Q4 气缸内气体 pV 关系 · 42/O/N/22 Q4 粒子动量与压强变化

Graphs

p–V graph — a curve falling to the right: pressure is inversely proportional to volume.
p–T graph — a straight line through absolute zero: pressure ∝ temperature (fixed volume).

Thermal Properties & Temperature

2.2

Definitions

Specific heat capacity (c) the energy required to raise the temperature of unit mass (1 kg) of a substance by 1 °C📌 考过 · 42/F/M/24 Q4 测油的比热容 · 43/M/J/24 Q4 算加热水所需能量 · 42/O/N/23 Q4 算水升温 20°C 所需能量.
Melting solid → liquid at a fixed temperature (0 °C for water); temperature remains constant while energy is absorbed.📌 考过 · 42/M/J/24 Q4 熔化时温度不变 · 42/O/N/24 Q4 高压锅——沸点与气压
Boiling liquid → gas throughout the whole liquid at a fixed temperature (100 °C for water at standard pressure); temperature remains constant while energy is absorbed.📌 考过 · 42/M/J/24 Q4 沸腾时温度不变 · 42/O/N/24 Q4 高压锅——沸点与气压
Evaporation the escape of the more energetic molecules from the surface of a liquid, at any temperature📌 考过 · 42/F/M/22 Q3 出汗降温 · 41/M/J/22 Q3 分子模型解释蒸发降温 · 42/M/J/26 Q5 蒸发降温与速率. It cools the remaining liquid (the faster particles leave, so the average kinetic energy falls).
Condensation / freezing gas → liquid / liquid → solid; energy is released.
Thermal expansion solids, liquids and gases expand when heated — gases expand most, solids least. Used in thermometers and bimetallic strips; expansion gaps are left in bridges and rails.📌 考过 · 43/O/N/22 Q4 温度计灵敏度与量程 · 41/O/N/24 Q4 热膨胀

Formulas

ΔE = mcΔθ energy transferred = mass × specific heat capacity × temperature change.📌 考过 · 42/F/M/24 Q4 E = mcΔθ 计算 · 43/M/J/24 Q4 E = mcΔθ 计算 Rearranged: c = ΔE ⁄ (m Δθ).

Graph

Heating curve — flat regions where melting and boiling occur: energy is absorbed without a temperature rise.
Evaporation — faster particles escape the surface; slower ones remain.
Boiling — bubbles form throughout the liquid; particles escape everywhere.

Transfer of Thermal Energy

2.3

Definitions

Conduction energy transferred through a solid by lattice vibrations (atoms/ions vibrate) and, in metals, by free (delocalised) electrons📌 考过 · 43/M/J/24 Q5 金属/非金属/气体的传导 · 41/O/N/23 Q2 铜锅——传导 · 43/M/J/25 Q4 铜锅——传导与比热容. Poor in gases — particles are far apart.
Convection energy transferred in a liquid or gas by density changes — hotter (less dense) fluid rises, colder (denser) fluid sinks📌 考过 · 41/M/J/24 Q3 冰在温水中——对流 · 41/M/J/25 Q4 暖气——对流 · 41/M/J/22 Q6 海陆风——辐射与对流.
Radiation infrared (electromagnetic) radiation emitted by all objects; travels through a vacuum — no medium is required📌 考过 · 42/F/M/26 Q4 户外取暖器——热辐射 · 42/F/M/25 Q3 电暖器——辐射 · 42/M/J/25 Q4 冷却容器——辐射.
Emitters & absorbers dull black surfaces are good emitters and absorbers of infrared; shiny white/silver surfaces are poor emitters and absorbers but good reflectors.
